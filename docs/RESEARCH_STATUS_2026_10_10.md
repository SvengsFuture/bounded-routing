# Documentation Status and Research Boundaries

Updated October 10, 2026. This is a navigation and status note, not a modification of any frozen specification or experimental verdict.

## Public repository scope

This repository investigates execution-time **route admissibility** under live constraints. It is not a general execution authorization framework, an LLM query router, or a production safety certification. Its recorded experiments cover the original routing series, independent structural evidence, rejected-configuration scars, cellular shedding, lineage inheritance, prospective filtering, and the V5 independent structural witness harness.

The [main README](../README.md) is the mechanism overview. [FILE_MAP.md](../FILE_MAP.md) is the file inventory. [ROUTING_VERDICT.md](../ROUTING_VERDICT.md) records the experiment verdicts. [The V5 harness README](../v5_independent_structural_witness/README.md) describes the executable evidence boundary and tests. [The EABC mapping](EABC_IP_BR_V5_001.md) is an architectural mapping, **not** an EABC conformance claim.

## Frozen results and claims

The existing V1 through V5 records remain historical artifacts. Their stated verdicts and evidence apply only to their declared workloads, assumptions, and tests. A successful synthetic harness does not establish production reliability, trusted physical state, independent hardware trust domains, or authorization of externally effective consequences.

V5's published mapping identifies open questions around **P5 state binding at externally effective commit** and **P7 end-to-end evidence correlation**. Neither is resolved by treating the signed observation record or internal replay watermark as proof of the external effect.

## Work after V5

Subsequent V6 discussions and adversarial review are exploratory. They must not be read as implemented V6 functionality or as an extension of V5's validation. The October 7 adversarial review rejected candidate shortcuts involving circular standing, dependency overlap, and treating provenance as sufficiency. Standing, provenance, and sufficiency remain separate questions. These findings do **not** constitute a validated V6 mechanism.

The October 9 FCR V6 research freeze is a separate research record. It is **not** incorporated here, and this public repository makes no new claim based on it.

## Publication boundary

Private FCR specifications, proofs, tests, and unpublished research records are not published by this status note. No FCR containment invariant or new V6 implementation is claimed here. Public summaries must not substitute for the original private records.

## Next evidence needed

Before any V6 capability is advertised as supported, it needs an explicit scope, defined predicates and inputs, a falsifiable validation plan, executable tests, and a frozen result record. The original V5 files and verdicts should remain unchanged.
